Privacy Policy

Athena and Archimedes · last updated October 24, 2026

Athena and Archimedes are personal tools used only by their owner, Devonte Hopkins. They are not offered to the public.

What they access

  • Gmail (read): Athena reads the owner's own email to summarize what matters to him, such as payments, bills and appointments.
  • Gmail (send): Athena and Archimedes send invoices from the owner's Gmail account, only after he approves each one.

Where the data stays

  • Email is processed on the owner's own computer and phone. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Invoices and client details (names, email addresses, phone numbers) are stored on the owner's own devices.
  • No email content is sent to third parties. Account numbers, passwords and verification codes are never sent anywhere.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings.
