Athena & Archimedes

Personal tools made by Devonte Hopkins (Merlin Interior Services, Edmonton).

Athena

A personal assistant that runs on Devonte's own computer. With his permission it reads his Gmail to tell him what matters (payments, bills, appointments) and sends invoices he has approved.

Archimedes

A phone app for his business: invoices, payments and expenses. It can send an invoice from his Gmail when he taps Send.

These are private tools for one person, not a public service. Privacy policy
